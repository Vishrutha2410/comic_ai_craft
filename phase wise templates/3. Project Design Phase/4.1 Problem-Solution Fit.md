# 4.1 Problem-Solution Fit

## 1. Customer Segment(s)
Primary users are non-artists, storytellers, hobbyists and casual users who want to turn a simple story idea into a complete comic. Students and creative users can also use the tool for personalized comic creation.

## 2. Jobs-to-be-Done / Problems
Users want to transform a simple prompt into a complete comic without needing professional writing or drawing skills. They need storyline, narration, character dialogue, illustrations, panel organization and a shareable PDF.

## 3. Triggers
- Having a story idea but not knowing how to turn it into a comic.
- Wanting a personalized comic with a chosen character, setting, tone and art style.
- Wanting a quick visual story that can be downloaded and shared.

## 4. Emotions: Before / After
**Before:** difficulty, uncertainty and frustration about creating a complete comic.

**After:** excitement, satisfaction and confidence after seeing the idea converted into a personalized comic.

## 5. Available Solutions
Current alternatives include manually writing and drawing comics, using separate story-writing and image-generation tools, or using conventional comic-creation software. These approaches may require more time or multiple tools.

## 6. Customer Constraints
Users may lack drawing or comic-design skills and may not have enough time to write, illustrate and arrange panels manually. They also depend on AI model/API availability and may need to review generated content for accuracy and consistency.

## 7. Behaviour
Users describe their story idea and customize the character, setting, tone and art style. They review the generated comic panel-by-panel and can download the final comic as a PDF for saving, sharing or offline viewing.

## 8. Channels of Behaviour

### Online
- Web application / browser interface
- FastAPI API endpoints
- Generated comic preview page
- PDF download

### Offline / Output
- Downloaded PDF comic
- Saved comic for sharing, printing or offline viewing

## 9. Problem Root Cause
The main difficulty is that comic creation combines several creative tasks: story planning, dialogue writing, scene description, illustration and layout. Doing all these tasks manually requires time, creativity and technical/artistic skills.

## 10. Your Solution
ComicCraft is a web-based AI comic creator. Users enter a story prompt, character name, setting, tone and art style. Gemini Flash creates a five-panel outline, Gemini Pro creates narration and dialogue, Stable Diffusion creates illustrations, and the system builds and exports the comic as a PDF.
